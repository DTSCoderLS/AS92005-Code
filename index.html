Welcome to Nature Heroes

Nature Heroes is a website created to inspire young people to protect the environment through the Maori concept of kaitiakitanga, meaning guardianship of nature. It shares easy ways to make a difference, like recycling, planting trees, and reducing waste. The goal is to show that small actions can help keep our planet healthy and make everyone a Nature Hero!
